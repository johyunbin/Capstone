### 1. 5 paradigm × 11 method framework (5/8 22:00 finalize)
| Paradigm | Inductive bias | Representative |
|---|---|---|
| **P1 Density Cluster** | "유사 데이터는 군집을 이룬다" | HDBSCAN, MiniBatch K-means, GMM |
